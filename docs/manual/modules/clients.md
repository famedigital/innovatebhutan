# Clients

**Route:** `/admin/clients`

## Purpose

Master record for every business you sell to or support. Jobs, invoices, AMC, and tickets hang off the client.

## Required fields (bible)

- **Name**
- **Phone** (not unique — shops may share numbers)

Optional: email, WhatsApp, WhatsApp group link, address, industry, notes.

## How to use

1. **Create** — Clients → Add / enroll. Enter name + phone first.
2. **Open hub** — click client → tabs for AMC, invoices, tickets, staff assign.
3. **Portal invite** — **Portal invite** button → enter email → copy link → send to client.
4. **Assign staff** — attach team members who work that account.

## Rules

- Prefer **archive** over hard delete (soft archive where available).
- Techs may create clients when taking a walk-in / WhatsApp lead (staff access).
- Clean data policy: re-enter important clients; do not bulk-import junk history.
